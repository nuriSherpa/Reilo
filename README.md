# Reilo

**Reilo** is a modern social platform for creating, sharing, and competing in reel-based challenges.  
It is built for people who want to showcase **real talent** through **raw, unfiltered videos** and grow by practicing consistently.

> **Be real. Do it again.**

---

## 🚀 What is Reilo?

Reilo is a reel-based challenge platform where users share authentic videos that highlight real skills — without filters, heavy edits, or artificial polish.

The platform focuses on:
- Consistency over virality  
- Growth over perfection  
- Skill over appearance  

Talent is built through repetition, not one perfect take.

---

## 🎯 Vision

Reilo helps users:
- Practice publicly  
- Compete through challenges  
- Track personal improvement  
- Build confidence through repetition  

Growth happens when you show up **again and again**.

---

## 🔑 Key Features (Planned)

- 🎥 **Raw Video Uploads**  
  No filters. No beauty effects. Just real performance.

- 🏆 **Challenge-Based System**  
  Daily and weekly challenges to encourage consistency.

- 📈 **Progress-Oriented Profiles**  
  See improvement over time through your own content.

- 👥 **Community Competition**  
  Compete fairly with others while focusing on self-growth.

- 🔒 **Authenticity First**  
  Designed to reduce fake engagement and promote real effort.

---

## 🧠 Meaning of “Reilo”

**Reilo** represents:
- **Re** – Repeat  
- **I** – Improve  
- **Lo** – Level up  

In short:  
**Repeat. Improve. Level up.**

## 🛠 Tech Stack (Initial Direction)

- **Frontend:** React / Next.js  
- **Backend:** Node.js / Express  
- **Media Processing:** FFmpeg  
- **Database:** PostgreSQL  
- **Storage:** Cloud-based object storage  
- **Authentication:** JWT / OAuth (planned)

*Tech stack may evolve as development continues.*
